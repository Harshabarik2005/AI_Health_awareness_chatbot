# Influenza and Common Cold

## Overview
The common cold and influenza (flu) are both contagious respiratory illnesses, but they are caused by different viruses. The flu is caused by influenza viruses only, whereas the common cold can be caused by a number of different viruses, including rhinoviruses, parainfluenza, and seasonal coronaviruses.

## Symptoms
While they share similar symptoms, flu symptoms are typically worse and come on more suddenly.

### Common Cold Symptoms
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Sore throat
- Sneezing
- Mild-to-moderate chest discomfort, cough
- Mild fatigue or weakness
- Rare to have a fever, headache, or extreme exhaustion.

### Influenza (Flu) Symptoms
- Fever or feeling feverish/chills (though not everyone with flu will have a fever)
- Cough (often dry and severe)
- Sore throat
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Muscle or body aches
- Headaches
- Fatigue (tiredness), which can be severe
- Some people may have vomiting and diarrhea, though this is more common in children than adults.

## Causes
- **Cold:** Usually rhinoviruses, but over 200 viruses can cause a cold.
- **Flu:** Influenza A, B, and sometimes C viruses.
Both are spread mainly by droplets made when people with the illness cough, sneeze, or talk. These droplets can land in the mouths or noses of people who are nearby.

## Risk Factors
- Age (children under 5, and adults over 65 are at higher risk for flu complications)
- Weakened immune system
- Chronic illnesses (asthma, heart disease, diabetes)
- Pregnancy
- Living or working in crowded conditions

## Complications
- Sinus and ear infections
- Pneumonia
- Bronchitis
- Worsening of chronic conditions (e.g., asthma attacks triggered by respiratory infections)

## Prevention and Treatment
### Prevention
- Annual flu vaccine (for influenza)
- Frequent hand washing
- Avoiding close contact with sick individuals
- Covering mouth and nose when coughing or sneezing

### Treatment
- Rest and hydration
- Over-the-counter medications to relieve symptoms (e.g., pain relievers, decongestants)
- Antiviral drugs for the flu (if prescribed early, such as Oseltamivir)
- Antibiotics do NOT work against viruses.
