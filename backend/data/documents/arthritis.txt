# Arthritis and Joint Pain

## Overview
Arthritis is the swelling and tenderness of one or more of your joints. The main symptoms of arthritis are joint pain and stiffness, which typically worsen with age. The most common types of arthritis are osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis.

## Types
- **Osteoarthritis (OA):** The most common type. It involves wear-and-tear damage to your joint's cartilage — the hard, slick coating on the ends of bones where they form a joint.
- **Rheumatoid arthritis (RA):** An autoimmune disorder in which the body's immune system attacks the lining of the joint capsule.
- **Gout:** A type of arthritis characterized by sudden, severe attacks of pain, swelling, redness and tenderness in one or more joints, most often in the big toe.

## Symptoms
Symptoms vary depending on the type of arthritis but generally include:
- Joint pain
- Stiffness, especially in the morning
- Swelling
- Redness
- Decreased range of motion

## Causes
- **Osteoarthritis:** Cartilage breakdown due to normal wear and tear, injury, or infection.
- **Rheumatoid arthritis:** An autoimmune response. The exact trigger is unknown, but genetics play a role.
- **Gout:** A buildup of uric acid crystals in a joint.

## Risk Factors
- Family history
- Age (risk of OA and RA increases with age)
- Sex (women are more likely to develop RA; men are more likely to develop gout)
- Previous joint injury
- Obesity (places extra stress on weight-bearing joints like knees and hips)

## Complications
Severe arthritis, particularly if it affects the hands or arms, can make it difficult to do daily tasks. Arthritis of weight-bearing joints can keep you from walking comfortably or sitting up straight.

## Management and Treatment
- **Medications:** Painkillers (acetaminophen, ibuprofen), Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), Corticosteroids, Disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs) for RA.
- **Therapy:** Physical therapy to improve range of motion and strengthen muscles surrounding joints.
- **Surgery:** Joint repair, joint replacement, or joint fusion.
- **Lifestyle:** Weight loss, low-impact exercise (swimming, cycling), applying heat and cold.
